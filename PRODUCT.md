# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (user's choice). No framework, no build step. Keep markup and assets portable so the page can later be moved into a Laravel Blade view without rework.

## Users

Four audiences, all confirmed as primary. The page must let each one find their own entry point:

- **University students and fresh graduates** who want to build a career in cybersecurity and land a job.
- **Beginners and career switchers** with no strong technical background, starting from zero.
- **IT professionals and developers** who want to specialize (Pentesting, SOC, Cloud Security) or prepare for certifications.
- **School students and teens** in intensive programs, in the spirit of apptrainers' existing Gaming Lab program, applied to cyber.

Arabic-speaking audience; English is also read.

## Product Purpose

apptrainers ("Translating ideas to reality through innovative education") runs cybersecurity courses. This landing page shows those courses and turns visitors into registrations through a form on the page. Success is a submitted registration from the right visitor for the right course level.

## Positioning

Confirmed differentiators, all four selected by the user:

1. **Hands-on labs.** Learners attack and defend in real environments, not slides.
2. **International certification prep.** (Security+, CEH, eJPT and OSCP were offered as examples in the interview. They are not yet confirmed per course.)
3. **Career path and employment.** Learners leave job-ready, with projects, a CV and training.
4. **Community and CTF competitions.** Capture The Flag challenges and a learner community.

Derived: one hands-on ladder that runs from a teen's first challenge to a professional's certification. A single-audience academy cannot truthfully claim that range.

## Operating Context

- Visitors arrive to evaluate courses and register. Registration happens on the page through a form. The backend will be connected later by the user.
- apptrainers already runs day-by-day intensive programs. The precedent is Gaming Lab: Unity game development, 30 hours over 10 days, 3 hours a day (`~/Downloads/Gaming Lab (Outline).pdf`). It shows the organization's program format. It does not confirm the format of any cyber course.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Bilingual page: Arabic is primary (RTL) and an English toggle is required. Technical terms (Pentesting, SOC, CTF…) stay in English inside Arabic copy.
- The primary action is a registration form on the page. Its submit target is not decided yet, so the form has to be easy to wire to a backend.
- **Undecided:** delivery format (in-person, online or hybrid) and city/location. The user said "later". Do not claim a format or a location.
- **Undecided:** the real course list, prices, schedules, durations and certification mapping.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **apptrainers**, lowercase wordmark. "app" is set in white inside a coral-red rounded square, followed by the "trainers" wordmark. Tagline: "Translating ideas to reality through innovative education".
- Brand red: `#EF4050`, sampled from the official SVG.
- Logo assets: `~/Downloads/AppLogo.svg` is the light-ground version, with "trainers" and the tagline tail in black. The user-supplied PNG is the dark-ground version, with "trainers" in white.
- The user made the logo binding. Asked what would make the design feel wrong, they pointed to the logo: the page must be faithful to this brand.
- **Standing visual preference (2026-09-24):** the user was shown two rounds of distinctive directions (a lock cutaway, a CTF board, a fight card, security printing, keycaps). They chose the category standard: a dark cyber-academy landing page. Convention is the commitment. The craft bar is **Hack The Box Academy**, executed at full fidelity in apptrainers' own brand (coral red replaces HTB's green), with no irony and no smuggled quirk.

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo in SVG and PNG form.
- No real course list, prices, graduate numbers, partners, accreditations or testimonials exist yet. At the user's request, all course content is authored as a realistic proposal, and every number, claim and name is marked for replacement. Never present invented statistics, partners, employers, accreditations or testimonials as real.

## Product Principles

1. **Every level finds its door.** Teen, beginner, student and professional each recognize their entry point within seconds.
2. **Prove it by doing.** Show practice (labs, challenges, flags captured), not promises.
3. **No invented claims.** Placeholders are visibly placeholders until real data replaces them.
4. **Arabic-first, bilingual by design.** Arabic reads natively in RTL. English is a full peer, not a machine afterthought.
5. **The logo leads.** Coral red `#EF4050` and the apptrainers wordmark anchor every decision.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Correct RTL and LTR mirroring in both languages, with `lang` and `dir` switched on toggle.
- Plain language that a teen or a complete beginner can follow. Jargon is introduced, never assumed.
